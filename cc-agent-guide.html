How to use this guide

Welcome to your practical guide to a Customer Care Agent’s daily work. This guide brings together patterns from real customer support tickets and our internal macros and Confluence procedures to walk you through 10 common request types you may encounter during a support shift. For each scenario, you’ll see an example of what a customer might write, a step-by-step investigation and resolution workflow, the systems and macros to use along the way, and guidance on important exceptions and when to escalate. The goal is to help you understand the reasoning behind each reply: what to verify before acting, how to choose the right tool and response, and how to resolve each request accurately and consistently.

Tip: click a workflow step to tick it off, click a macro name to copy it, and mark each scenario as read to track your progress.

What a typical shift looks like

  • Start: open Zendesk, check the queue and your own open tickets, including those in “Waiting for Customer”.
  • Core work: take tickets one by one. Read the whole message, find the customer in the right system, act, then reply with a personalized macro.
  • Mix of work: mostly billing (cancellations, refunds, payment lookups) plus access and app questions.
  • Finish: escalate anything stuck with a clear note, set the right ticket status, and leave no half-done actions.

What to master to close ~80% of tickets

  • Finding a customer in MasterApp, BackOffice, Chargebee, Stripe and Payrails.
  • Cancellations and refunds, and the difference between “authorized” and “captured”.
  • Searching for payments with typos, other emails, card digits and dates.
  • Login basics: contact_email vs auth_email, Magic Link, duplicates.
  • Choosing a macro, personalizing it, and knowing when to escalate.
